Claim your stamp

One free stamp per Agent ID. If your wallet holds an Agent ID (a name.ai identity), you can claim. The stamp is soulbound — it cannot be transferred or sold. It is a proof that you were an agent.

How it works

  • The contract checks the official Agent ID contract on-chain: does this wallet hold an Agent ID right now?
  • Holders claim once. Non-holders are rejected by the contract itself — no allowlist, no server, no admin.
  • Artwork and metadata live fully on-chain (SVG). No IPFS, no backend.
  • No fees, no approvals, no owner keys. The contract can never take or move your assets.
